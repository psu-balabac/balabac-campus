About the Campus

Higher education serving southern Palawan.

Palawan State University – Balabac Campus is an external campus located in Balabac, Palawan. It helps bring accessible and quality higher education closer to students and communities in the southernmost municipality of Palawan.

The campus advances the university's commitment to education, research, innovation, extension, and community-oriented services that contribute to sustainable development and improved quality of life.
